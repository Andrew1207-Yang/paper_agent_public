# Summary

Describes a latent-state modeling system for proposing candidate scientific hypotheses.

# Core Idea

The system searches latent transitions and maps surprising trajectories back to human-readable hypotheses.

# Weaknesses

- The paper blurs the line between hypothesis generation and validation.
- The examples are intriguing but not very rigorous.

# Open Questions

- Can latent simulations be evaluated without overfitting to known discoveries?
