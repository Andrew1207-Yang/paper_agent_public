# Scoring Rubric

Use scores from 1 to 5.

- `importance`: How important the paper is to the broader research direction.
- `taste_match`: How closely the paper matches current research interests.
- `research_idea_potential`: How likely the paper is to spark follow-up ideas.
- `technical_depth`: How technically rich or rigorous the paper appears.
- `urgency`: How soon the paper should be read.
